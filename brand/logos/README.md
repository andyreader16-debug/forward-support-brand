# Logos

Real asset files only. Never synthesise the Forward Support logo.

## In here

- `logo-blue.jpg` — primary blue-on-white master, 896 x 644px. The canonical mark.

## Outstanding

- `logo-white.png` — reversed white-on-dark variant. Required for document covers, section dividers, business card backs, dark hero website surfaces. Brand Book Section 14 lists this as outstanding since 28 April 2026. The master JPG must not be colour-inverted to fake one.

## Rules (Brand Book Section 02)

- Minimum size: 25mm wide in print, 100px wide on screen.
- Clear space: height of the letter F in FORWARD.
- Never stretch, distort, rotate, or change colour.
- Never recreate in code, SVG, ASCII, or by any other means.
- Never crop or obscure.
